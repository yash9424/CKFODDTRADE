# Photo credits

Photography sourced from Wikimedia Commons for the hero sections of this site.
Only licences permitting **commercial use and cropping** were used — CC0,
Public Domain and CC BY. ShareAlike, NonCommercial and NoDerivatives images
were excluded.

Client-supplied photography in `public/images/photos/` (rice, spices, fresh
produce, and the Dubai port shots taken by CK Foodstuff) is not listed here and
carries no attribution requirement.

## Attribution required (CC BY)

Under CC BY you must credit the author, name the licence and link back to the
source. The simplest compliant option is a credits line in the website footer
or a `/legal/photo-credits` page.

## Before launch — please action

1. **CC BY attribution is a licence condition, not a courtesy.** Six of the
   seven hero photographs require a visible credit naming the author, the
   licence and a link to the source. Add a credits line to the footer or a
   `/legal/photo-credits` page. Without it the site is not compliant.

2. **`dairy-pasture.jpg` is a Michigan farm, not a European one.** It appears
   on `/products/dairy`, which is headed "European Dairy Sourcing". The photo
   makes no location claim itself and shows Holstein cattle in pasture, but if
   that pairing feels misleading, replace it with a European dairy photograph.

3. **Consider licensed stock instead.** These are the best commercially-usable
   images available on Wikimedia Commons, but a paid stock library (Getty,
   Adobe Stock, Stocksy) would give stronger, on-brand Dubai and food-trading
   photography with no attribution obligation at all.
